# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is split evenly between potential freelance clients looking for a design/development partner and creative agencies evaluating Kenneth for a permanent design/developer role.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Kenneth Jørgensen's selected work, capabilities, point of view, and contact path. Success means that either audience can quickly understand the quality and breadth of his work, inspect relevant projects, and start a conversation.

## Positioning

Kenneth works across visual design and front-end development, combining creative direction with the ability to build the resulting experience.

## Operating Context

Visitors typically arrive while comparing designers, developers, or hybrid creative technologists. They need to scan the work quickly, understand Kenneth's role in it, and find a direct contact route.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The portfolio is bilingual in English and Norwegian.
- Existing project routes, page transitions, responsive behavior, and contact functionality must continue to work.
- The site uses React, TanStack Router, GSAP, React Three Fiber, and Sass.
- The current WebGL aurora background is a defining technical and atmospheric asset.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the name Kenneth Jørgensen.
- Preserve the aurora background and incorporate the aurora borealis as a core identity element.
- Draw structural and interaction inspiration from The Shift Tokyo while remaining an original portfolio.
- The voice should feel direct, capable, and internationally relevant rather than sales-heavy.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing project entries and case-study routes in `src/app/components/Layout/Project/Projects.tsx` and `src/app/project/`.
- Existing project imagery and video in `public/images/` and `public/videos/`.
- Existing aurora/WebGL implementation in `src/app/components/Experiences/`.
- No testimonials, client metrics, or employment claims are available and none should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Let finished work lead the experience.
- Give freelance clients and creative employers equal clarity and weight.
- Make Kenneth's hybrid design-and-development value immediately understandable.
- Keep exploration expressive while keeping contact and project navigation obvious.
- Preserve speed, accessibility, and reduced-motion support alongside visual ambition.
