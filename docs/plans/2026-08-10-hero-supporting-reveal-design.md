# Hero supporting reveal

Remove the upper metadata rule and let the title remain the hero's clear opening gesture. The supporting role, statement, description, project link, and environment values enter as one continuation of that gesture: a short left-to-right sequence combining a small horizontal offset with a soft blur that resolves into place. The sequence overlaps the tail of the character reveal so the hero feels continuous rather than divided into separate animation phases. Reduced-motion behavior remains immediate and fully visible.
