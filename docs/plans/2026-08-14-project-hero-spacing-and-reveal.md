# Project hero spacing and reveal

Increase the project hero's top breathing room, reduce the title scale, and
spread the title and role across the full case-study width. Keep the continuity
overlay aligned by deriving its destination from the same responsive spacing
and type values used by the hero CSS.

On continuity arrivals, keep the heading row hidden until the image handoff is
complete. Then reveal the title with a restrained blur, opacity, and vertical
settle; allow the role to follow with a short stagger. Direct and reduced-motion
arrivals remain immediately readable.
